# SchweisOS Brand Assets

License identifier: LicenseRef-SchweisOS-Brand-Assets

The SchweisOS logo and project marks are official SchweisOS brand identifiers.
They are distributed so official SchweisOS systems can identify themselves
consistently in desktop environments and system information tools.

These assets are not a substitute for technical value, and they do not change
the licensing of upstream Arch Linux or third-party software.

## Covered Assets

This license applies to the SchweisOS logo and icon files installed by the
`schweisos-branding` package, including:

- `/usr/share/schweisos/branding/schweisos-logo.png`
- `/usr/share/icons/hicolor/1024x1024/apps/schweisos.png`
- `/usr/share/pixmaps/schweisos.png`

The same terms apply to the canonical source logo at
`branding/assets/logo/schweisos-logo.png`.

## Permissions

You may copy and redistribute the covered assets, without modification, as part
of official SchweisOS source trees, packages, installation media, release
artifacts, documentation, screenshots, and mirrors that distribute unmodified
official SchweisOS artifacts.

You may also use the covered assets, without modification, to refer to
SchweisOS in truthful documentation, reviews, compatibility notes, issue
reports, and screenshots.

Technical resizing, file format conversion, and icon-cache processing are
allowed when they preserve the visual design and are necessary for desktop icon
lookup, packaging, documentation, or accessibility.

## Conditions

All allowed uses must:

- preserve this license notice or a reasonable reference to it;
- avoid implying endorsement, certification, or official release status for an
  unofficial build, remix, fork, mirror, package, or derivative;
- avoid using the SchweisOS name, logo, or marks as the primary branding for an
  unofficial operating system or software distribution.

## Restrictions

You may not create modified logos, confusingly similar marks, alternate
official-looking release artwork, or derivative brand identities from the
covered assets without explicit written project approval.

You may not use the covered assets to distribute malware, credential-harvesting
software, deceptive installers, unsigned artifacts presented as official
SchweisOS releases, or artifacts that weaken SchweisOS package trust policy.
