# SchweisOS Brand Assets

The SchweisOS logo and project marks are official SchweisOS brand identifiers.
They are distributed so official SchweisOS systems can identify themselves
consistently in desktop environments and system information tools.

These assets are not a substitute for technical value, and they do not change
the licensing of upstream Arch Linux or third-party software.

Until a complete trademark and brand-use policy is published, reuse of the
SchweisOS name, logo, and marks should avoid implying that an unofficial build,
mirror, remix, or derivative is an official SchweisOS release.
