# SchweisOS Code of Conduct

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-SA-4.0

SchweisOS is a technical project, but it should also be a humane place to work.

## Standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and direct.
- Argue from evidence, tests, documentation, and maintainability.
- Welcome users and contributors who are still learning.
- Disclose uncertainty instead of overstating confidence.
- Respect user privacy and security.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, architecture, and behavior.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Deceptive technical claims.
- Hidden data collection or attempts to bypass user consent.
- Pressure to accept unclear security, privacy, or maintenance tradeoffs.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these standards.

As the community grows, SchweisOS should revisit this document and define a
more formal enforcement and reporting process.
